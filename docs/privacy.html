Privacy Policy

7 Seas News Bot · last updated November 17, 2026

The 7 Seas News Bot is used only by the owner of 7 Seas News to publish the page's own news videos.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the 7 Seas News YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, and it does not access data of any other person.

Storage

The access permission (refresh token) is kept encrypted as a private secret of the owner's GitHub repository and is used only to upload the page's own videos. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: send a message to the 7 Seas News Facebook Page.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
